Live passive scanning

The live scanner watches every response recorded by the proxy and applies the passive rules to it in real time. It sends no extra traffic of its own — it only reads what your browser already fetched. Findings appear in the Findings view as they are produced. Live scanning is off by default and honours the project scope.

When traffic arrives faster than the scanner can process it, the overflowing responses are parked in a durable backlog (on disk) and drained on idle. Nothing is silently dropped: every parked row is counted, and the backlog survives a process restart so scan coverage is never lost to a crash.
